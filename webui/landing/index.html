ROAD GRAPH ENGINE
Vehicles move on a structured urban road network. Shortest paths are computed using Dijkstra's algorithm over real topology.

CLOSED-LOOP LIFECYCLE
Vehicles transition through Moving → Assigned → Charging → Completed. Station occupancy is locked to prevent conflicts.
